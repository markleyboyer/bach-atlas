Scope, dates, and significance

Catalogue coverage: 1,548 individual entries, variants, reconstructions, and separately listed parts from IMSLP’s public works table, including every main BWV number 1–1179 through a work or version entry, plus 199 historical appendix entries. This is an exploration index, not a complete transcription of the scholarly BWV third edition. Individual movements are not comprehensively indexed. Older Anh. numbers can duplicate works reassigned to the main catalogue.

Dates retain the source’s uncertainty and revision ranges. Chronological grouping uses the earliest four-digit year listed; undated works stay separate. A decade group does not establish a precise composition date. Instrument families are broad groupings derived from the source’s forces; the original abbreviations remain visible on each work.

Stars and diamonds are editorial listening and study priorities, based on historical influence, musical scope, and enduring repertoire presence—not objective rankings. Collection membership gives its entries the collection’s marker. A famous work can have disputed authorship, as with BWV 565. “Not flagged in source” does not certify authenticity.

Sources: IMSLP works catalogue · Bach Archive: BWV 1178–1179 · Netherlands Bach Society: performances. Compiled 7 October 2026.

Forces: org = organ; kbd = keyboard; hpd = harpsichord; vn = violin; vc = cello; viol = viola da gamba; fl = flute; ob = oboe; bn = bassoon; str = strings; bc = basso continuo; vv = voices; ch = choir; orch = orchestra.
