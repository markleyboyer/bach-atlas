# Data sources and provenance

## Catalogue

Source: https://imslp.org/wiki/List_of_works_by_Johann_Sebastian_Bach

Retrieved 7 October 2026. The index uses factual work identifiers, titles, scoring, keys, and dates, with status indicators derived from source notes. Extended source commentary is not reproduced. See the source for its current attribution and licensing information. Grouping, normalization, and significance selections are additions to the source data.

Includes 1,349 main-catalogue entries and 199 historical BWV Anh. entries. Every integer BWV number 1–1179 is represented by a work or version entry. Aggregate collection rows are represented through grouping rather than duplicated as individual entries. Individual movements are not comprehensively indexed.

BWV 1178 and 1179: https://www.bacharchivleipzig.de/en/bach-archiv/newly-discovered-organ-works-johann-sebastian-bach

## Recordings

Official source: https://www.bachvereniging.nl/en/bwv/

All of Bach is the Netherlands Bach Society's ongoing recording project. Performance links and video identifiers are extracted from its official work pages. The `checked` field records the retrieval date. Performer credits and movement labels are factual metadata. No recordings or images are redistributed.

Excerpt boundaries use the first non-menu chapter listed on the performance page, capped at 60 seconds. Without a chapter list, the excerpt starts at the video's beginning. Samples are recognition aids; musical selection is not individually curated. A recorded work can have multiple performances or movements, and recordings are matched to exact source BWV identifiers where possible.

YouTube embed parameters: https://developers.google.com/youtube/player_parameters
